Parametric Product Design & Pricing

See the full loss distribution and tail behavior of a parametric product before you commit capital to it.

  • Trigger and index design through payout structure, exhaust, and limit — with policy wording that pays cleanly when the trigger fires.
  • Burn cost, AAL, and stress testing under historical and stressed climate scenarios, with explicit loadings.
  • A Python pricing prototype you keep and re-run as your data updates, not a black box.

Actuarial Modeling Modernization

Your experience studies and valuation runs stop being a quarterly fire drill — refreshes run in hours on governed, versioned data.

  • Spark SQL and cluster tuning that cut runtime and cloud spend on billion-record, trillion-dollar fund-value pipelines.
  • MLflow versioning so every model run is reproducible and auditable for the regulator and the model-risk committee.
