Mortality-Curve Visualizer

How different is a law-based fit (Gompertz–Makeham) from a GLM spline on the same data?

This tool is in build. Want early access or a fit on your own experience data? Request a demo →

Build spec

Tier
1 — client-side; curve fitting is a candidate for Pyodide.
Inputs
Bundled sample age / exposure / deaths dataset, plus a toggle of fitted curves.
Compute
Gompertz–Makeham law fit vs. GLM spline fit on qx.
Output
Overlaid mortality curves (observed vs. two fits) with residuals.
Done when
Both fitted curves overlay the observed points on one chart; no upload needed for v1.
